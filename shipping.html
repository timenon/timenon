Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2025

Free Delivery — Uyo, Akwa Ibom

All orders delivered within Uyo, Akwa Ibom State are shipped completely free of charge. No minimum order value applies for Uyo deliveries. Orders are typically delivered within 1–2 business days.

Free Delivery — Orders Over NGN 100,000

Any order with a subtotal of NGN 100,000 or above qualifies for free delivery anywhere in Nigeria, regardless of the delivery address.

Standard Delivery — Rest of Nigeria

For orders outside Uyo, live delivery rates are calculated at checkout based on your address. We partner with trusted Nigerian logistics providers including GIG Logistics and Kwik Delivery. Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days depending on your location.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 24–48 hours of payment confirmation. You will receive a WhatsApp notification when your order has been dispatched with tracking information.

Packaging

All Timenon orders are carefully packed to ensure your items arrive in perfect condition. Our packaging reflects the premium nature of the brand.
